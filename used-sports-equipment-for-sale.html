Used sports equipment for sale

Our used equipment is bought from local players, schools, and clubs, then inspected and cleaned before it goes on the floor. Below is a look at what you will usually find in the shop and how we decide what is good enough to sell.

What we carry

Cycling and wheels

Road bikes, hybrids, mountain bikes, helmets in good standing, and in-line skates. Every bike gets a brake, chain, and tire check.

Golf and racquet sports

Iron sets, drivers, putters, tennis racquets, pickleball paddles, and badminton gear. Grips are replaced when worn.

Team and fitness gear

Baseball gloves, basketball shoes, hockey skates, adjustable dumbbells, and kettlebells at a fraction of new prices.

Our inspection promise

Marcus Ortega personally reviews every item. He looks for cracked frames, frayed straps, worn bearings, flattened padding, and loose hardware. Helmets older than five years, and any helmet that has taken a hard impact, are never put up for sale because their protection can no longer be trusted.

If something you buy turns out to have a defect we missed, we will repair it, replace it, or refund you within 14 days.

Want to sell your gear?

We buy clean, working equipment in good condition. Bring your items to the shop during open hours and we will make an offer on the spot, or email photos to hello@secondwindsports.example for a quick estimate. You can take cash or choose store credit, which comes with a 15 percent bonus.

Looking for a better price? Check our discount sports equipment page, or visit our kids' gear section for growing athletes.
