Sell used sports equipment for cash or credit

Have gear sitting in the garage that nobody uses anymore? Second Wind Sports Exchange buys clean, working sports equipment from local players, families, schools, and clubs. We make you an offer on the spot, and you choose whether to take cash or store credit.

How selling works

1. Bring it in

Visit the shop during open hours with your gear. You can also email photos to hello@secondwindsports.example for a quick estimate first.

2. Get an offer

Marcus Ortega, our head equipment inspector, checks each item and we give you an offer on the spot. There is no fee and no obligation to accept.

3. Cash or credit

Take cash today, or choose store credit and we add a 15 percent bonus to the offer.

What we buy

We buy clean, working equipment in good condition, including bikes, golf clubs, tennis racquets, pickleball paddles, skates, baseball gloves, team gear, and fitness equipment such as dumbbells and kettlebells.

We do not buy broken items, gear with missing parts, or helmets that are more than five years old or have taken a hard impact. Those helmets can no longer protect the person wearing them, so we cannot resell them.

Tips for a better offer

Clean it first. A wiped-down glove or a washed pair of cleats looks better and sells faster, which raises what we can offer.

Bring everything that goes with it. Bags, covers, pumps, and original straps all add value.

Sell in season. Skates and hockey gear are in demand in the fall, while baseball gloves and bikes sell fastest in spring.

Selling kids' gear? Ask about our trade-in credit on the used sports gear for kids page.

Ready to shop instead?

Once your gear is sold, you can use your credit to buy used sports equipment from our shelves. Questions about the process? Our frequently asked questions page has answers, or call us at (555) 010-0142 Tuesday through Saturday, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., or Sunday from noon to 4 p.m.
