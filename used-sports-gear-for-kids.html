Used sports gear for kids

Children outgrow cleats, gloves, and pads long before they wear them out. That makes used gear one of the smartest buys a parent can make. Our kids' department is run by Priya Natarajan, a former youth coach who knows what young players actually need and what they can skip.

Popular kids' gear

Cleats and shoes

Soccer, baseball, football, and basketball footwear in youth sizes 10C through 6Y, checked for sole wear and loose stitching.

Pads and protection

Shin guards, hockey pads, and catcher's gear. We only sell protective items that still meet fit and condition standards.

Starter sets

Ready-to-play bundles for first-time players: a glove, bat, and ball, or a youth bike with a helmet check.

Sizing and safety tips

Leave room to grow, but not too much. Cleats should have about a thumb's width of space past the longest toe. Gear that is too big can cause blisters and trips.

Helmets get extra scrutiny. We inspect shells, straps, and padding on every youth helmet. Any helmet that shows cracks or has taken a hard hit is removed from sale.

Try before you commit. Bring your child in to test the fit. Priya will help with sizing, and our 14-day return window covers growth spurts that happen faster than expected.

Grow-Up Trade-In program

When your child outgrows their gear, bring it back. We offer store credit based on condition, which you can put toward the next size up.
