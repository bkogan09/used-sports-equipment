Discount sports equipment

Used gear is already well below retail, but we also run regular markdowns so you can find cheap sports equipment at even better prices. Here is how our discounts work and when to shop for the best deals.

Three ways to save

Sports equipment clearance rack

Items that have been on the floor for 45 days drop an extra 25 percent. After 90 days they drop 50 percent. Cheap never means unsafe: clearance items pass the same inspection as everything else we sell.

Starter bundles

Buy a complete set, such as a beginner golf bag with clubs or a tennis racquet with a bag, and take 15 percent off the total.

Loyalty credit

Join our free Second Wind Club and earn one dollar of credit for every twenty dollars you spend.

Tips for getting the best price

Buy out of season. A used snowboard in June costs noticeably less than the same board in November.

Ask about Fair-grade items. Scuffs and wear rarely affect performance, and Fair pieces carry our deepest everyday discounts.

Bundle your family's needs. If you are buying for more than one player, ask Dana Whitfield or a team member to build a custom bundle.

Know what not to discount-hunt. Safety items like helmets and mouthguards are only sold when they pass inspection, and we will never mark down their safety standards.
