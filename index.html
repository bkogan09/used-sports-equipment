Quality used sports equipment, priced for real life

Second Wind Sports Exchange is a family-run shop that buys, inspects, and resells pre-owned sports equipment. Founded by Dana Whitfield after she spent a small fortune outfitting her two kids for three seasons of soccer, baseball, and hockey, we believe nobody should pay full retail for gear that will be outgrown in a year.

Every item that reaches our shelves is checked by hand, cleaned, and honestly described. If a glove is stiff, a helmet has a scuff, or a racquet needs new grip tape, we tell you before you buy.

Why shoppers choose us

Inspected by a person, not a checklist. Marcus Ortega, our head equipment inspector, spent twelve years as a team equipment manager. He tests straps, seams, welds, and padding on every item.

Plain-language condition grades. We label items Like New, Good, or Fair, and we explain exactly what each label means on every tag.

14-day return window. If the gear does not fit or feel right, bring it back in its original condition for store credit or a refund.

Better for the planet. Each piece of gear we resell is one less item headed to a landfill.
