Frequently asked questions

New to buying used sports gear? These are the questions we hear most often from shoppers at Second Wind Sports Exchange. If you do not see your question answered, call us at (555) 010-0142 or email hello@secondwindsports.example.

Buying used gear

Is used sports equipment really safe?

Yes, when it has been inspected properly. Marcus Ortega, our head equipment inspector, checks every item for cracks, frayed straps, worn bearings, and flattened padding. Anything that fails is repaired or removed from sale.

Do you sell used helmets?

Only in limited cases. We never sell a helmet that is more than five years old or has taken a hard impact, because its protection can no longer be trusted. Helmets that do pass get an extra inspection of the shell, straps, and padding.

What do your condition grades mean?

Like New means used only a handful of times with no visible wear. Good means light cosmetic wear and fully functional. Fair means clear wear or scuffs but works as intended. You can see typical savings for each grade on our used equipment for sale page.

Can I try gear before I buy it?

Absolutely. Swing the club, test the glove, or bring your child in to check shoe and pad sizing. Our team is happy to help you find the right fit.

Prices and discounts

How much cheaper is used gear than new?

Most items sell for 30 to 75 percent less than the price of buying new, depending on condition and demand.

When do prices drop even further?

Items that stay on the floor for 45 days drop an extra 25 percent, and after 90 days they drop 50 percent. We also run seasonal markdowns each month. See the full schedule on our discount sports equipment page.

What is the Second Wind Club?

It is our free loyalty program. You earn one dollar of store credit for every twenty dollars you spend. Ask at the register to join, or email us with your name.

Returns, payment, and pickup

What is your return policy?

You have 14 days to return gear that does not fit or feel right. Bring it back in its original condition for store credit or a refund. If an item has a defect we missed, we will repair it, replace it, or refund you within that same 14 days.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, major credit and debit cards, and Second Wind store credit.

Do you ship?

We ship small and medium items, such as cleats, gloves, racquets, and pads, anywhere in the contiguous United States for a flat rate of $9.95. Bikes, golf bags, and other large items are available for in-store pickup only.

When are you open?

Tuesday through Saturday, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., and Sunday from noon to 4 p.m. We are closed on Mondays.

Selling and trading in

What gear do you buy?

We buy clean, working equipment in good condition, including bikes, golf clubs, racquets, skates, team gear, and fitness equipment. We do not buy broken items, damaged helmets, or gear with missing parts.

How do I sell my gear?

Bring it to the shop during open hours and we will make an offer on the spot. You can also email photos to hello@secondwindsports.example for a quick estimate.

Is cash or store credit better?

That is up to you. If you choose store credit, we add a 15 percent bonus to the offer.

How does the kids' trade-in program work?

When your child outgrows their gear, bring it in for store credit based on condition, which you can put toward the next size up. Priya Natarajan, who runs our kids' department, can walk you through it. Typical credit amounts are listed on our used sports gear for kids page.

Teams and groups

Do you offer discounts for teams, schools, or leagues?

Yes. Coaches and league organizers can ask about 10 percent off orders of ten or more items. Email us with your team name and the gear you need, and Dana Whitfield will put together a quote.
